# Hi, I'm Geethanjali Rathlavath

I'm a **Healthcare Data Analyst** with 4 years of experience turning medical and pharmacy claims, member
eligibility, provider, EHR, and HIM data into numbers people can trust. I'm currently a Data Analyst at
**Cigna Healthcare** (January 2025 – Present).

Most of my work sits between the data and the decision: finding out *why* a cost moved or a claim was denied,
building the SQL and Power BI reporting that answers it, and validating every figure before it reaches a
stakeholder.

**What I work with**

- **SQL**: queries, stored procedures, and views; SQL Server, Snowflake, Azure SQL
- **Power BI**: dashboards, KPI scorecards, and interactive reports
- **Python**: automated validation and reconciliation workflows
- **ETL**: SSIS, dbt, SAS-to-SQL migration, validation across DEV, QA, and PROD
- **Healthcare data**: claims, eligibility, utilization, Epic and Allscripts EHR data, ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS

**Credentials:** AHIMA Certified Health Data Analyst (CHDA) · Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst
Associate (PL-300) · M.S. Health Data Analytics, University of North Texas
